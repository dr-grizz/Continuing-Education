Your achievements will appear here.

After a course awards a badge, you can view it here, download it and choose whether to share it.

You control what you share.

Badges stay private until you enable a verification link. Anyone with that link can view the achievement, issuer, issue date and current status; your learner ID, grades and course evidence are excluded.

Turning sharing off makes the verification page unavailable; it cannot recall a credential someone already downloaded or copied.

These pilot credentials use the CU signed envelope format; Open Badges wallet compatibility is not yet available.
