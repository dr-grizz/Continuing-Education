## Interpretation and limits

See the [financial definitions](../earnings.md) and [ADR 0022](../adr/0022-earnings-dashboard.md). Stripe sandbox and simulated records are separate test sources. Receipts after refunds deduct current cumulative refunds against selected original payments; they do not represent profit, settlement, or refund-by-issue-date cash flow. Checkout outcomes use sessions created in the selected period and their current matched-payment status. No fees, costs, payouts, taxes, card-decline history, production financial reconciliation, or load-test qualification is claimed.
