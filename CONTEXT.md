# Continuing Education domain context

- **Course product:** instructional subject and reusable catalog description.
- **Offering:** saleable instance/cohort with dates, capacity, approval rule and optional stable Canvas mapping. Imported never-published drafts may lack required details and cannot publish/register until complete. The prototype stores sample course descriptions directly on offerings; product normalization is deferred.
- **Seat capacity:** a limit of 1–10,000 or an explicit Unlimited seats choice. Unlimited is stored separately from unset imported-draft capacity, preserves eligibility/approval/payment rules, and does not consume a finite seat pool. A new finite limit cannot fall below seats already in use.
- **Course type:** `cohort` uses scheduled start/end dates; `self_paced` means learn on your own and start anytime, with no cohort dates. User-facing labels are **Scheduled** and **Self-paced**; show **Start anytime** for self-paced courses. Course type is distinct from delivery format and access-duration policies.
- **Staff member:** a portal staff user in the `staff_members` directory. Members have one or more roles and are deactivated rather than deleted. Sign-in supports configured Canvas OAuth or the local mock mode. Optional API read scopes allow their Canvas authorization to read course metadata.
- **Progress requirements version:** immutable per-offering step definitions; learners are explicitly assigned a version independently of registration/payment, and missing evidence remains awaiting verification.
- **Learner cohort:** explicit named membership tied to one progress requirements version; individual evidence is shared across cohort reports, and removing membership preserves individual history.
- **Completion evidence:** an append-only documented staff verification or correction, with learner-visible note/reference and the staff actor; no automatic Canvas completion sync is implied.
- **Credential:** an earned badge stored in the portal PostgreSQL database and managed inside its API, optionally with a frozen certificate snapshot/PDF attached; distinct from a portal entitlement.
- **Credential claim distribution:** a recipient-bound, expiring QR/typed-code route to an already-issued badge, its attached certificate, or both; redemption acknowledges receipt without changing eligibility, ownership or signed award content.
- **Certificate email delivery:** a staff-reviewed, recipient-bound outbox record for one frozen certificate; cohort sends create separate messages, provider acceptance is not verified inbox delivery, and uncertain outcomes require an explicit reviewed resend. Automatic email is separately configured and off by default.
- **Badge account link:** an explicit private mapping of an active portal learner UUID to an approved badge-service learner ID; not email matching.
- **Badge manager:** a staff member holding `badges:manage` (course manager or staff administrator), authorized by the current portal session.
- **Assessed skill alignment:** a canonical, versioned O*NET skill definition plus the institution's explanation of assessment, frozen with an approved badge and its issued accomplishment; occupational relevance alone is not learner mastery.
- **Career reference:** publicly accessible O*NET occupation information and BLS wage/projection estimates, labeled by source, period and geography; independent of private learner records and award eligibility.
- **Staff role:** approver, course manager, support, IT/Canvas administrator, staff administrator or content editor. Each role grants permission scopes; endpoints check scopes, not role names. The Finance role is not supported; the university Finance department works outside the portal.
- **Learner:** internal portal person (Support can deactivate an account, which blocks sign-in and learner actions while existing paid enrollments continue); the prototype persists one demo learner per normalized email in PostgreSQL. Existing shared sample registrations remain on an email-less legacy learner. Sign-in supports explicit local mock mode or existing CU accounts through identity-only Canvas OAuth. Canvas learners bind to installation/user ID and use separate revocable sessions; email may be unset and never links demo history automatically. No passwords are stored. Future guest accounts are created only after confirmed payment through the approved institutional workflow.
- **Entitlement:** learner-to-offering access justification, distinct from payment and identity status.
- **Approval validity:** how long an approval lets the learner reserve a seat (default 7 days, set per offering). Lapsed or declined approvals can be requested again.
- **Enrollment window:** Scheduled courses accept registration until a set number of days after the start date (default 7, set per offering). Self-paced courses never close.
- **Canvas offering mapping:** one default offering per Canvas installation URL and course ID. Explicit selected-course sync creates an unpublished draft, refreshes source metadata and preserves all staff-owned offering fields. Conflicting existing links require review; mapped links are protected.
- **Offering display image:** Canvas imports follow the course image with a CU CE fallback unless staff explicitly select a portal upload or CU CE image. Portal image choices are versioned, audited and preserved by source refreshes; only an explicit reset follows Canvas again. No portal edit changes Canvas.
- **Canvas course link:** per-offering link that learners see only after their Canvas account and enrollment are complete.
- **Price history:** append-only record of an offering's prices. A reservation keeps the price quoted when the seat was reserved, and payment charges that quoted price.
- **Offering listing:** `listed` is independent from publication. Published/listed offerings appear in public discovery; published/unlisted offerings accept direct-link registration under the same eligibility rules. Unlisted is shareable, not private access. Public collections/pathways and their aggregates exclude unlisted courses; staff views and learner-owned saved/paid records remain available. Existing offerings default to listed, and unpublish/republish and Canvas source refresh retain the preference.
- **Collection/pathway listing:** independent of group publication and member-course listing. Published unlisted groups open by direct link but are excluded from public group discovery, browse filters and course recommendations. Listing changes retain membership, ordering, featured picks and private pathway follows; they never change individual offerings.
- **Unpublished offering:** removed from the catalog. No new registrations, approval requests or payments; enrollments already paid continue.
- **Order:** payment record. Prototype orders are explicitly simulated, not financial evidence.
- **Operation:** durable workflow with a queryable status. In the current demo, normal simulated fulfillment completes during payment; delayed/failed stages resume on authenticated requests with bounded retries. There is no always-running worker.
- **Exception:** a paid registration whose provisioning failed after automatic retries. It waits in the staff queue until staff retry provisioning (after the cause is fixed) or record a manual enrollment, each with an audited reason. Learners see “Support review needed” and are never asked to pay again. In the prototype, failures are simulated by a staff-selected outcome.
- **Migration/cutover:** project transition process, not portal business behavior.

Portal owns catalog and workflow coordination; IT owns identities; Canvas owns learning access/records; Finance owns payment/refund decisions. Prototype adapters must never call production services. Issue creation requires explicit user instruction.
